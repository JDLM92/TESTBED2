Quality Checklist

  • Match camera position, height, and horizon exactly.
  • Preserve key buildings and the skyline for continuity.
  • Keep lighting direction and time of day consistent.
  • Use realistic scale for people, bikes, buses, and trees.
  • Respect the 2:1 equirectangular format (no stretched poles).
